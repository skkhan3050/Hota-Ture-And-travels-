Privacy & Policy

Your privacy and data security are very important to us at Hota Tour & Travels.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a tour enquiry or contact form on our website, we may collect information such as your name, mobile phone number, email address, proposed travel dates, number of travellers, and specific travel preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information provided is strictly used to:

  • Process your travel enquiry and send personalized tour quotes.
  • Coordinate cab pickups, hotel bookings, and travel logistics in Puri & Odisha.
  • Communicate essential updates regarding your booked services.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

Hota Tour & Travels respects your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. Personal data is shared only with verified local service partners (e.g. hotel reception, assigned driver) strictly required to fulfill your itinerary.
